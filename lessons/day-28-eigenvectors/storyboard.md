# Day 28: Eigenvectors and eigenvalues

The whole video uses one matrix, Lay's first example $A = \begin{bmatrix} 3 & -2 \\ 1 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$, with its
columns green (where $\mathbf e_1$ lands) and red (where $\mathbf e_2$ lands), as on Day 5. The plane is centred a
little below the middle of the frame with 1 scene unit per grid step, so the image of $(-1, 1)$ at $(-5, -1)$ still
fits. Ordinary inputs are purple-gray and outputs are teal. The two eigen-directions have fixed colors for the whole
lesson: the line through $\mathbf v_1 = (2, 1)$ with $\lambda = 2$ is yellow, and the line through
$\mathbf v_2 = (1, 1)$ with $\lambda = 1$ is blue. Orange is only for the small glowing marks on tips that stay on
their line. The moving grid is the image of the integer grid under $(1 - t)I + tA$, so every point slides along a
straight path, and a vector that $A$ only stretches stays on its line for the whole move.
