# Day 10: What is a vector space?

The lesson reuses Day 1's arrows v = (3, 2) in yellow and w = (−1, 2) in blue for the opening. In the centerpiece
the polynomial p(t) = 1 + 2t − t² is yellow and q(t) = 2 − t + t² is blue, with their sum teal, so each curve on
the left wears the same color as its coefficient arrow on the right. The right panel draws coefficient space
(axes labelled 1, t, t²) through a fixed oblique view with a faint floor grid and drop lines for depth.
