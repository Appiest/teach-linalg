Day 32 is diagonalization: when a matrix has enough eigenvectors, you can use them as a new grid, and on that grid the matrix only stretches each axis. That turns into A = PDP⁻¹, which makes a big power like A¹⁰ take two products instead of nine. In the video, watch the grid straighten out, stretch, and swing back, and see the arrow land exactly where A sends it.
