# Day 34: Symmetric matrices and the spectral theorem

The video follows Lay's $A = \begin{bmatrix} 7 & 2 \\ 2 & 4 \end{bmatrix}$ (§7.1 Example 4), whose unit eigenvectors are
$\mathbf u_1 = (2, 1)/\sqrt5$ with $\lambda = 8$ and $\mathbf u_2 = (-1, 2)/\sqrt5$ with $\lambda = 3$. As on Day 28, the
eigenline with the larger eigenvalue is yellow and the other is blue. The unit circle is purple-gray and its image is
teal. Orange marks only the right angle, the tips and the active factor. The plane is centred at (0.3, 0.35) with
0.68 scene units per grid step, so the ellipse with half-lengths 8 and 3 fills the frame without touching the caption.
The moving grid follows $(1 - s)I + sA$, which for a symmetric $A$ keeps the same eigenvectors the whole way, so the
eigenvector arrows stay on their lines while the circle stretches.
