Day 18 is up! Days 16 and 17 gave every matrix two subspaces, and today asks how their sizes are related. Every column either adds a dimension to what comes out or gets crushed to zero, so rank plus nullity always equals the number of columns, and you'll watch a whole cube of inputs flatten into a plane while one line shrinks into the origin.
