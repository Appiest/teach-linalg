# Day 14: Basis

The lesson works in $\mathbb{R}^2$ so every grid stays readable. The standard basis $\mathbf e_1, \mathbf e_2$ keeps
the green and red of $\hat\imath$ and $\hat\jmath$. The basis $\mathcal B$ uses yellow $\mathbf b_1 = (2, 1)$ and
blue $\mathbf b_2 = (-1, 1)$, the main and second vector colors. The spare vector $\mathbf u = \mathbf b_1 + \mathbf
b_2 = (1, 2)$ is pink. The point $\mathbf x = (1, 5)$ is a teal dot throughout, since it is the result every recipe
builds. The second skewed basis $\mathcal C$ uses pink $\mathbf c_1 = (3, 1)$ and purple-gray $\mathbf c_2 = (-1, 2)$.
The polynomial section reuses Day 10's $\mathbf p(t) = 1 + 2t - t^2$ in yellow.
